Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 July 2026

Canadian Hockey Wraps respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how you can contact us about your data.

Information We Collect

When you request a design proof or contact us, we may collect your name, email address, selected package, number of pairs, finish, installation preference, estimated subtotal, and any details you choose to include about your skates or design idea.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, prepare a design proof, provide a quote, arrange installation or shipping where relevant, and keep a record of customer communication.

Form Submissions

Our quote form is processed through Formspree. When you submit the form, the information you enter is sent to us by Formspree so we can reply to your request.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website. This may collect information such as pages visited, device/browser information, approximate location, and referral source. This helps us improve the site and understand which parts of it are useful.

Cookies

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to measure website usage. You can control or block cookies through your browser settings.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us run the website, process quote forms, provide analytics, or complete your order.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep enquiry and order-related information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you, provide our services, keep business records, and meet any legal or accounting requirements.

Your Rights

You can ask us to update, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to any records we need to keep for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or your personal information, contact us at info@canadianhockey.co.uk.
