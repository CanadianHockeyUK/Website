Good to know.

Will wraps damage my skates?

Wraps are designed to be removable from clean, intact boot surfaces. Older damaged finishes may lift.

Can you match team colours?

Yes. Send your colour references, jersey photos, or existing brand files and we will proof the match.

Do you install them?

We offer DIY wrap kits for those who prefer to customize their skates at home. Please be aware, however, that applying skate wraps requires precision and patience to achieve a perfect finish.

If you would prefer to leave it to the experts, we offer a professional installation service for an additional £35. You can either visit us in person, or ship your skates to our workshop. If you choose to mail them in, please allow one week for us to complete the installation and return them to you.

Refunds and returns

Because every set of skate wraps is custom-printed just for you, we aren't able to accept returns once print production has started. We take great care in the creation of your custom gear, and we want to ensure you love the final product—so please double-check your order details before finalizing your purchase!
